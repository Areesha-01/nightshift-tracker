# NightShift — User Guide

This guide explains how to use the NightShift Task & Bug Tracker as a registered user.

## 1. Creating an Account

Go to the **Register** page and fill in your name, email, and password.

- **Registering as an Employee:** Leave the admin code field empty. Your account will be created but will need approval from an admin before you can log in.
- **Registering as an Admin:** Enter the admin registration code provided by your team lead. Your account is created as an admin and is automatically approved — no waiting required.

## 2. Logging In

After logging in, you'll be taken to one of three places depending on your account:

| Account Status | Where You Land |
|---|---|
| Employee account, not yet approved | **Pending Approval** screen |
| Employee account, approved | **Dashboard** |
| Admin account | **Admin Dashboard** |

If you see the Pending Approval screen, contact an admin to approve your account. You won't be able to access the task board until then.

## 3. Employee Dashboard

As an employee, you can:

- **View the task board** — tasks are organized into columns (To Do / In Progress / Done)
- **Change a task's status** — drag a task card to a different column, or use the status dropdown on the task
- **Add comments** to any task — useful for updates, questions, or notes for the team

You cannot create, edit, delete, or assign tasks — this is managed by admins.

## 4. Admin Dashboard

As an admin, you have full control over the task board plus a user-approval panel:

### Managing Tasks
- **Create a task** — click "Add Task", fill in the title (required), description, priority, due date, and assignee, then save
- **Edit a task** — click on a task card to update its details
- **Delete a task** — remove tasks that are no longer needed
- **Assign a task** — pick a team member from the assignee dropdown
- **Move tasks** — drag and drop between columns, same as employees
- **Comment** — add comments on any task, same as employees

### Approving Employees
- Open the **pending users** panel on your dashboard
- Each pending account shows the person's name, email, and when they registered
- Click **Approve** to let them into the system, or **Reject** to remove the request
- Once approved, that employee can log in and access the Dashboard immediately

## 5. Task Priorities and Due Dates

Tasks can be marked **Low**, **Medium**, or **High** priority, and can have an optional due date. These are set by admins when creating or editing a task, and are visible to everyone on the board so the whole team can see what's urgent.

## 6. Comments

Anyone (admin or employee) can add a comment to a task. Comments are timestamped and show who wrote them — useful for leaving updates without changing the task itself.
